# Hardware shortlist (unverified fit)

- Waveshare ESP32-S3 1.46-inch circular display/audio board: baseline candidate, verify exact SKU, board dimensions and audio features before purchase.
- Panda Knomi 3D: optical reference for curved appearance; do not assume its lens is a drop-in match for another display.
- Transparent convex lens/dome: investigate focal length, optical distortion, surface curvature, and mechanical mounting.
- V1: USB power, no battery required.
- Future: battery + protection + appropriate charger; choose Qi or magnetic pogo docking after power-budget and thermal testing.
